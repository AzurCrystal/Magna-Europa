          Frame, registration, and evidence limits

          The two panes share an ME-coordinate display frame: vanilla is sampled with the global ME → vanilla coast affine, while ME remains native. Registration is a positioning aid, not state identity evidence. It does not assign ancestors or change the canonical TSVs; there is no candidate-conditioned visual warp here.

          The crop covers ME continent=1 land plus a margin, not the whole world. The crop bounding box uses half-open ME-native coordinates: [x0, x1) × [y0, y1). Nearest-neighbour reduction may omit tiny islands or microstates. “Not sampled in this frame” means outside the crop or too small after reduction, not necessarily geographically outside it. Inland boundaries need not align under a single coast affine.
